# WORLD GENERATOR

You build ONE room, then you are done. This prompt runs exactly one time,
before the first player action exists.

Nothing else exists yet - no other rooms, no characters, no facts. The world
grows later, out of what the player actually does (see the resolver). Do not
try to plan ahead or imply a larger structure here; build only what is
asked.

--------------------------------------------------
1. LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------

The player wrote this start prompt:

$START_PROMPT$

The language of that text is the language of this game. Report its BCP-47
tag (de, en, fr, ...) in the field that asks for it.

Every text the player will read must be written in that language: the room
name, the opening narration. The room's anchor and the image prompt stay
English - working material for the machine, not part of the fiction.

--------------------------------------------------
2. THE ROOM
--------------------------------------------------

start_node_name: a short place name, in the language of the start prompt.

start_node_anchor: the room's permanent physical description, in English.
Write only nameable, touchable things that will still be true in twenty
scenes: materials, structures, dimensions, what is fixed in place.

    good:  low concrete vault, one pump housing, water on the floor
    bad:   an oppressive place where something terrible happened

No mood. No events. No people - people move, anchors do not. No exits: this
room has none yet. What happens later arrives as marks, and the first way
out is whatever the player's own first action creates - not something you
invent here.

--------------------------------------------------
3. THE OPENING SCENE
--------------------------------------------------

Scene 1 is narration only.

No player action has happened yet. Nothing here may react to one - no "you
decided", no "after you", no consequence of a choice that was never made.
Establish where the player stands and what can be seen from there.

Suggest, through what is described, at least one plausible direction to go
- a doorway, a gap, a sound from somewhere unseen - WITHOUT that direction
being an actual exit yet (there is none in this room's data). The player's
own first move is what turns that suggestion into a real, persistent place.

End in a position from which acting is obviously possible, without listing
options.

opening_image_prompt: English, place/light/materials/perspective, nameable
physical things only. No style words - the visual style is applied by the
client. No negations: an image model cannot draw the absence of something.
No action, no text in the image.
